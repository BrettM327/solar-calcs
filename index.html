Step-by-Step Procedure
  1. 1Locate the initial Contract Signature Date — this determines the Dealer Fee expiration (+180 days from signing).
  2. 2Set the PPL Rate in the header — check Domestic Content or Energy Community if applicable (30%), otherwise it stays at 25%.
  3. 3Confirm the Baseline / Redline PPW for the applicable state and product from your pricing reference.
  4. 4Check the finance portal for the correct Dealer Fee % for the product and loan term. Enter as a decimal (e.g. 18.25% = 0.1825).
  5. 5Enter GSP, Mod Wattage, and Mod QTY from the most recently signed Contract, Change Order, or EPC document.
  6. 6Enter all Adders from the most recent Construction Review, PM, or AM assessment. The Dealer Fee is calculated separately — do not include it in the adder list.
  7. 7For PPL deals, use the PPL Calcs tab to determine the correct GSP and split. For deals with a roof bundled in, use the Roofing HIC tab to compare structuring options.
PPL Rate Rules
25% (Default)Standard rate. No special qualifications apply. Customer pays 75% of GSP.
30%Applies when the project qualifies for Domestic Content OR is located in an Energy Community. Check either box in the header. Customer pays 70% of GSP.
The PPL rate set in the header automatically updates all applicable calcs across every tab — PPL Calcs, Pricing tab banners, and Roofing HIC options.
Product Types
LoanStandard loan. Dealer fee applies to full GSP. Net PPW = (GSP − Adders − DF) / System Size.
CashNo dealer fee. Net PPW = (GSP − Adders) / System Size.
TPOMonthly rate + escalator. No dealer fee. Use the escalator table to model total cost over term.
Pre-Paid LeaseLoan with dealer fee. The PPL company covers 25% (or 30%) of GSP upfront. Customer pays the remainder via cash or Credit Human loan.
Pre-Paid Lease+Same as PPL with a 30% customer refund (5% at install, 25% at PTO). Uses ≈23.08/76.92 fixed split. Customer can apply refund to the loan or take as cash.
Roofing HIC — Option Summary
Pre-Paid Lease tab compares 4 structuring options when a roof is bundled into a solar deal:
Option 1Split HIC & PPL, keep the same PPW. Loan increases slightly to compensate for PPL lost on the HIC portion.
Option 2Split HIC & PPL, $0 loan increase. PPW decreases slightly.
Option 3Swap with fixed loan (alt GSP back-solve). PPW decreases more than Option 2.
Option 4Full HIC transfer. Entire roof cost moves to HIC. Loan and PPW increase significantly.
Pre-Paid Lease+ tab compares 2 options:
Option 1Keep same loan/PPW. Customer refund decreases.
Option 2Keep same customer refund. Loan increases to compensate.
